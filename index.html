Glory stays gentle. These settings are saved on this device.

Graphics quality Low is the phone default: smaller shadows, no ambient occlusion. High adds SSAO and a whisper of depth of field. Changing this reloads the Terrace.
Text size Dialogue, captions, and whispers.

In conversation, Tab or the arrow keys move the gold focus ring. Enter chooses. Keys 1–4 still work. A gamepad left stick walks, the right stick looks, and the south button talks.

Hybrid Launch Architecture & UE5 Roadmap

Two complementary tracks bringing Heaven Encounter to players worldwide.

Track 1 — Live Browser Vertical Slice (You Are Playing It!)

Hosted on GitHub + Cloudflare Pages with zero downloads required. Readers of The Waiting Room of the Saints and viewers of the 43-minute film can immediately walk the Terrace, breathe the Healing Leaves, and experience 360° Guided Visions right in any browser.

Full cinema-grade fidelity powered by Lumen, Nanite, Niagara, MetaHumans, and MetaSounds across all 7 Zones—from the Arrival Meadow and Weaving Grounds (Corinne) to the Treasuries (Dimming Gold), Eastern Gate of One Pearl, Street of Transparent Gold, and Sapphire Sea of Glass.
