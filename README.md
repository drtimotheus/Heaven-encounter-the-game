# Heaven Encounter: The Game

**Playable 3D Browser Vertical Slice — *The Terrace of New Arrivals***  
*Grounded in "The Waiting Room of the Saints: A Novel of Heaven" by Timothy Holt (2026).*

## About the Experience

*Heaven Encounter: The Game* flips the open-world sandbox formula from violence and chaos to **healing, restoration, honest fellowship, and awe**. This live browser build serves as the interactive 3D Vertical Slice prototype and Visual Art Bible showcase ahead of the flagship **Unreal Engine 5** desktop release (itch.io → Steam & Epic Games Store).

### What You Can Explore in This Vertical Slice
- **Zone 1 — The Arrival Meadow & Fork of the Gray Mist:** Walk across living grass that bends and springs back greener, beneath iridescent flora and prismatic birds trailing light. Stand at the unchained edge of the Gray Mist—a quiet witness to free will.
- **Zone 2 — The Terrace of New Arrivals & Crystal River of Life:** Cross the golden arched stone bridge or wade effortlessly through the luminous River of Life.
- **Zone 3 — The Walled Orchard:** Gather and breathe the **Leaves of the Tree of Life**, sit with **Mara** and **Rev. Thomas Ainsley**, and hear honest stories of shame and striving healed by grace.
- **Zone 5 — The Golden Pool of Living Memory:** Step into **3 playable 360-degree Guided 3D Visions**:
  1. **Creation (*"Let There Be Light"*)** with Amos
  2. **The Cross (*"The Wound That Became a Gate"*)** with Elias Vance
  3. **Testimony Echo (*"The Boy in the Sawdust"*)** with Elias Vance
- **Diegetic "Growing Eyes" Progression:** Engage with testimonies and visions to lift the veil on the **Rising Road Overlook**, beholding the distant **Eastern Gate of One Pearl** and **Sapphire Sea** horizon.
- **Visual Art Bible & UE5 Roadmap:** Browse all 15 cinematic reference plates mapped directly to Unreal Engine 5 zones, MetaHumans, and shaders.
