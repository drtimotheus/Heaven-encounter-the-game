# Asset credits

Heaven Encounter uses these textures, lighting images, and character models. All of them allow use in a game.

## Poly Haven (CC0 1.0)

- HDRI: `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` (1k), for image-based lighting. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
- `leafy_grass` diffuse, normal (GL), and roughness. https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass
- `coast_sand_01` diffuse and normal (GL). https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01
- `marble_01` diffuse, normal (GL), and roughness. https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
- `bark_willow` diffuse and normal (GL). https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow
- `oak_wood_planks` diffuse and normal (GL). https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_wood_planks

## ambientCG (CC0 1.0)

- `LeafSet017` color and opacity, combined into `leaf_set.png` for the orchard canopy. https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet017

## tevy.dev characters (CC0 1.0)

Rigged humanoids with idle, walk, and wave clips. Low quality uses LOD1; Medium and High use LOD0. https://tevy.dev

- Casual Walker, the youth face (Paul, David)
- Road Worker, the sturdier face (Peter)
- Dog Walker, the older face (Amos, Elias, Thomas, John, Moses, Elijah, Isaiah)
- Hiker, the women's face (Mara, Mary Magdalene)

Modern clothes on those bases are hidden. Each saint wears a linen robe, colored trim, and their own hair or veil, built in this project. `linen.png` is an original weave.

## tevy.dev props (CC0 1.0)

- Seagrass Patch and Wildflower Patch, instanced as the meadow
- Lady Fern
- Weathered Ionic Column, used for the colonnade and the Pearl Gate

The files use `KHR_texture_basisu` and `EXT_meshopt_compression`. The Basis transcoder shipped in `public/basis/` comes from three.js (MIT).

## Original

- `water_ripples.png` is a gentle ripple normal written for this project and released with the game.
- Paul’s bald crown is a shader on the Casual Walker, plus a gray fringe, so the demo keeps him short and balding.
